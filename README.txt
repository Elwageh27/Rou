Dental Exam Lab — English Exam Version
========================================
1) Extract the ZIP file.
2) Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3) Works offline. Keep index.html and questions.js in the same folder.

Question language:
- All MCQ questions and answer choices are in English.
- All Enumerate prompts and model answers are in English.
- Explanations and corrective feedback for wrong MCQ answers are in Arabic.
- Enumerate answers are shown as an English model answer for self-checking.

Includes MCQ practice, Enumerate practice, topic filters, wrong-question review,
and a mixed 20-question MCQ exam. Questions are based on the three supplied lecture PDFs.
